# Hook Patterns

A field guide to opening lines that earn the click. Each pattern has a
Chinese and an English example. Mix and match — never stack more than two.

## 1. The Question
End the first line with a genuine question.
- 中文：`为什么你的帖子总是没人看？`
- EN: `Why do your posts get zero replies?`

## 2. The Number
Lead with a specific number. Specificity is credibility.
- 中文：`我用 30 天把账号从 0 做到了 600 粉`
- EN: `I grew from 0 to 600 followers in 30 days`

## 3. The Bold Claim
A contrarian take, stated plainly. Must be defensible.
- 中文：`说个暴论：正常人是做不好 X 的`
- EN: `Unpopular opinion: normal people can't do X well`

## 4. The Curiosity Gap
Start the story, withhold the payoff.
- 中文：`昨天一条帖子爆了，但原因跟我想的完全不一样`
- EN: `A post blew up yesterday — for the opposite reason I expected`

## 5. The How-To
Promise a concrete outcome.
- 中文：`3 步写出高互动帖子，新手照抄就行`
- EN: `3 steps to high-engagement posts — beginners can copy this`

## 6. The Data Lead
Borrow credibility from numbers.
- 中文：`统计了 100 条爆款帖，发现它们都有这 3 个共同点`
- EN: `Analyzed 100 viral posts — they share these 3 traits`

## 7. The Story Cold Open
Drop into a moment. No setup.
- 中文：`凌晨 2 点，我删掉了那条 10 万阅读的帖子`
- EN: `At 2am I deleted the post with 100k views`

## Anti-patterns

- Leading with a hashtag or @mention
- "大家好" / "Hey guys" openers — pure throat-clearing
- ALL CAPS, emoji stuffing, or engagement bait ("转发送 XX")
- Burying the point below 3 lines of context
